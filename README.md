# FiniZen

Smart, simple, and secure. Track, save, plan, and achieve your financial goals while keeping your money life Zen. It is quite crude but gets the job done… Made with the simple goal, to manage my cash in college.

## Getting Started
Copy the relevent files, run flutter pub get on cmd to get started on the app.

## Features
-  **Track your income and expenses** — stay on top of every rupee.
-  **Manage bills and due dates** with timely reminders.
-  **Categorize transactions** for easier financial insights.
-  **View simple spending summaries** to understand your habits.
-  **Built-in To-Do list** to track financial goals and tasks.
-  **Dedicated savings page** to monitor and maintain savings targets.
-  **Create local backups** of your data for peace of mind.
-  **App lock feature** to keep your data secure.
-  **Goofy-Aah profile picture customizer** for a bit of fun and personality.
